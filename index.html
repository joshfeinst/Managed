The mouse plays the whole game. Click a patch of floor to walk there, click a machine or a person to walk over and use them, click to advance anything. Nothing needs the keyboard except typing a job posting ID.

Your terminal (the glowing one at your desk) opens the service queue. The shift clock runs the whole time you are out on the floor — walking, or standing still deciding what to do next — and each job also charges its own minutes the moment you start it. It stops only behind a panel: a conversation, the queue, a minigame, the pause menu. So reading is free and dithering in the corridor is not. The printer is possessed. Welcome aboard.
